# Legal & Compliance Review Checklist — Creator Marketplace

> **Part 19.** An **implementation-oriented checklist for counsel**, not legal advice and
> **not legal conclusions**. Every area marked 🔴 **requires professional legal review before
> commercial launch**. This module is documentation-only; nothing here authorizes operating.

Legend: 🔴 counsel required before launch · 🟠 counsel recommended · ⚪ operational/design.

## Worker & contracts
- 🔴 **Contractor classification** — creators/contractors must not be misclassified;
  platform framing, control, and payment terms affect classification (varies by
  jurisdiction). Interacts with OQ-24.
- 🔴 **Creator terms of service** — engagement terms, deliverable acceptance, revision,
  rejection, non-payment conditions, dispute/appeal rights.
- ⚪ **Not payroll/employment** — Partnera does not run payroll; employee compensation is out
  of scope ([PRODUCT_DEFINITION.md](PRODUCT_DEFINITION.md#3-money-taxonomy--precise-distinctions)).

## Content ownership & rights
- 🔴 **Content ownership** — default ownership vs. license (D-320/D-335); what the business
  receives on approval; what the creator retains.
- 🔴 **Licenses** — scope, exclusivity, geography, permitted platforms, duration; downstream
  affiliate use must stay within license.
- 🔴 **Moral rights** — attribution/integrity rights where applicable.
- 🔴 **Music rights** — licensed-music requirements; liability for unlicensed tracks.
- 🔴 **Model releases** — people appearing in content.
- 🔴 **Location releases** — filming locations/venues.
- 🔴 **Trademarks** — brand-mark use, impersonation, third-party marks.

## Disclosure & advertising
- 🔴 **FTC (and equivalents) disclosure** — sponsored/paid content must be disclosed;
  disclosure blocks are first-class in the page builder.
- 🔴 **Affiliate disclosures** — existing affiliate obligations extended to creator-content-
  driven promotion.
- 🔴 **Sponsored-content disclosure** — creator obligations surfaced in briefs/terms.

## Money, tax & sanctions
- 🔴 **Payment regulations / money transmission** — non-custodial stance (D-050/D-106) is the
  hinge; escrow-like states must not constitute improper custody
  ([PAYMENTS_AND_FEES.md](PAYMENTS_AND_FEES.md)).
- 🔴 **Tax reporting** — creator earnings reporting (1099/DAC7-style); thresholds; documents.
- 🔴 **KYC / KYB** — identity/business verification thresholds (OQ-22).
- 🔴 **Sanctions / AML** — screening; prohibited jurisdictions (OQ-23).

## People & privacy
- 🔴 **Minors** — minimum creator age (D-334/OQ-21); minors depicted in content.
- 🔴 **Privacy** — controller/processor stance (inherits D-113); creator PII, submission
  content, retention/deletion ([CONTENT_ACCESS_SECURITY.md](CONTENT_ACCESS_SECURITY.md)).
- 🔴 **Biometrics** — faces/voices in content; biometric-privacy laws (e.g. BIPA-style).

## AI & automated decisions
- 🔴 **AI review disclosure** — inform creators AI-assisted review is used
  ([AI_REVIEW_ARCHITECTURE.md](AI_REVIEW_ARCHITECTURE.md)).
- 🔴 **Automated-decision appeal** — GDPR Art. 22-style rights for any automated approval/
  rejection (OQ-26); appeal path designed, legal scope open.

## Platform liability & operations
- 🟠 **Platform liability** — intermediary/safe-harbour posture; content-safety obligations.
- 🔴 **Disputes** — process, windows, binding effect, arbitration/venue.
- 🔴 **Refunds** — business/creator refund and reversal terms.
- 🔴 **Record retention** — money/audit retention vs. privacy deletion; lawful hold for
  disputes/fraud/CSAM preservation.

## Launch gating
- Commercial launch (CM16) and pilot (CM15) are gated on counsel sign-off of: creator terms,
  disclosures, license defaults, custodial stance, KYC/tax, supported jurisdictions, minors,
  and AI-decision rights. See [IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md](IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md).

> This checklist identifies areas for review; it makes no determination on any of them.
