# 14 — Affiliate Portal

> The Affiliate Portal is the experience for **affiliates and partners** — the people promoting businesses. It must make it effortless to get links/coupons, understand performance, and get paid. Clarity and trust are everything here.

## Audience

Affiliates/creators (and, in partnership contexts, partner representatives) — scoped to the programs they belong to.

## Sections

### Home & programs
- **Dashboard** — earnings snapshot, recent conversions, balance, alerts.
- **Programs** — programs they're in; discover/join open or marketplace programs.
- **Applications** — status of programs applied to.

### Promote
- **Referral Links** — generate/manage trackable links.
- **Coupons** — assigned discount codes for attribution.
- **Campaigns** — active campaigns they can join/promote.
- **Promotional Material** — creatives/assets provided by businesses.
- **Announcements** — updates from businesses/platform.

### Performance
- **Clicks** — traffic they've driven.
- **Conversions** — attributed conversions & basis.
- **Orders** — underlying orders (as permitted).
- **Performance** — trends, best links/campaigns, tier progress.

### Money
- **Pending Earnings** — commissions not yet available (maturing/held).
- **Available Balance** — withdrawable.
- **Withdrawals** — request payouts; see thresholds/status.
- **Payment History** — past disbursements (immutable record).

### Account
- **Notifications** — status changes, payouts, announcements.
- **Settings** — profile, payout methods (tokenized), identity/tax info, security.

## Cross-cutting expectations

- **Transparency:** every commission is explainable (which order, which offer, why) — reduces disputes and builds trust.
- **Clear money states:** pending vs. available vs. paid vs. reversed are unambiguous ([06-commission-engine.md](06-commission-engine.md)).
- **Honest tracking:** affiliates can see their attributed activity and understand attribution basis.
- **Payout clarity:** thresholds, schedule, holds, and identity/tax requirements shown up front ([07-payments-payouts.md](07-payments-payouts.md)).
- **Multi-program:** one affiliate identity can span many businesses' programs.
- **Fraud-aware:** holds/reviews are communicated respectfully with reasons where appropriate.

## Design priority
Responsive and approachable — affiliates are often on mobile. (Admin/Business are desktop-first; the portal leans friendlier/simpler.) UI stack not chosen here.
